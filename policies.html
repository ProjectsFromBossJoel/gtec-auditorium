Key Points

What You Need to Know

A summary of the most important policies before booking a hall at GTEC.

Advance Booking

All bookings must be made in advance and confirmed by our team before the event date.

Guidelines Mandatory

Clients must download and read the usage guidelines before submitting a booking request.

Hall Conditions

Halls must be returned in their original condition. Damage may attract additional charges.

Catering & Setup

External catering is permitted. Setup and breakdown times must be agreed in advance.

AV & Technical Support

On-site AV technicians are available to assist with equipment setup and livestreaming.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations must be communicated in writing. See the full policy document for details.
