Our Story

A Premier Hub for Professional Events

GTEC Conference Facility is an institution dedicated to providing world-class conference venues for businesses, educational institutions and organisations. Our mission is to offer convenient, reliable, and professional spaces to host events of any scale.

From intimate boardroom sessions to large-scale academic conferences, we provide the infrastructure, technology and support your event deserves. Our facilities are designed with the modern professional in mind — combining comfort, functionality and aesthetic excellence.

Located in the heart of Accra, Ghana, GTEC has become the preferred partner for institutions and organisations seeking a dependable, high-quality event venue.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide accessible, secure and professionally supported spaces for learning, meetings and events — empowering every client to host with confidence and distinction.

Our Vision
