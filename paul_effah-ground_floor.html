About This Hall

Paul Effah Hall (Ground Floor) is a large, modern auditorium ideally suited for conferences, academic examinations, graduation ceremonies and corporate events. As the ground-floor section of GTEC's premier Paul Effah Hall, it provides a self-contained, fully equipped venue for events of up to 320 attendees.

The hall features professional LED and mini screens, a powerful PA system with excellent acoustics, full air conditioning and high-speed connectivity — delivering the same premium standard as the full auditorium in a more focused capacity setting.
